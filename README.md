# KJLR_17_2
A Study on the Semantic Network and Topic Modeling of Joseon Dynasty Gyehoesi Using Text Mining

This study quantitatively analyzes Joseon-period Gyehoesi – poems composed for social gatherings – using text mining techniques. It examines the symbolic structures and communicative patterns of gatherings among the Joseon literati through semantic network analysis and topic modeling. To this end, approximately 470 Gyehoesi were collected from the Comprehensive Corpus of Korean Literary Collections (Hanguk munjip chonggan), and an analytical corpus was constructed by preprocessing the original texts. Subsequently, unigram and bigram text-mining analyses were conducted to identify recurring keywords and semantic patterns. 
The unigram keywords revealed a semantic structure organized around four dimensions: documentary functions, communal solidarity, public character, and commemoration. Meanwhile, the bigram keywords formed semantic domains centered on aesthetic sociability (pungnyu), commemorative and record-keeping practices, collective bonding, and temporal consciousness. Furthermore, a category-based analysis demonstrated that private Gyehoesi foregrounded friendship and the appreciation of natural scenery, whereas official Gyehoesi were organized around terminology concerning official posts, organizations, and institutions.
The unigram semantic network analysis revealed that Gyehoesi was structured around three primary axes: commemoration and visualization, sociability and bonding, and temporality and assembly. Moreover, the bigram topic modeling of official Gyehoesi showed distinct discourse structures associated with major government offices, including the Office of the Inspector General (Saheonbu), Office of the Censors (Saganwon), Royal Secretariat (Seungjeongwon), and State Tribunal (Uigeumbu). These structures corresponded, respectively, to inspection, remonstrance, attendance at the royal court, and judicial administration. 
By quantitatively identifying the linguistic patterns and latent discourse structures of Joseon Gyehoesi through text mining techniques, this study empirically confirms that these poems functioned as a medium for collective communication. Rather than being merely instances of individual literary expression, Gyehoesi served as a cultural mechanism that reinforced social cohesion among the Joseon literati (sadaebu) and strengthened the institutional identities of the offices involved. Thus, this research sheds new light on the communal culture of Joseon society and the social role of literature.

Keywords : Gyehoesi, Text Mining, Semantic Network, Topic Modeling, Digital Humanities
